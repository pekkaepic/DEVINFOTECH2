Our Story & Background

Bridging the Gap in Premium Computing

At Dev Infotech, we specialize in certified refurbished corporate-grade laptops and authentic software tools. We deliver reliable computing power to students, professionals, and small businesses with complete transparency.

Brand new commercial laptops like Lenovo ThinkPads, Dell Latitudes, and HP EliteBooks often cost upwards of ₹70,000 to ₹1,20,000. Many buyers settle for low-end plastic consumer laptops that deteriorate within a year or two.

Our mission is to make robust, enterprise-built machines accessible at a fraction of their original cost—tested, certified, and backed by a comprehensive seller warranty.

Precision Engineering

Our 4-Stage Diagnostic Process

How we transform corporate-grade machines into certified, reliable daily drivers.

Corporate Sourcing

We select high-tier corporate lease return machines from reputable enterprise environments with verified original chassis.

30+ Point QC

Comprehensive diagnostic testing: CPU stress tests, SSD health verification, RAM integrity checks, display pixel audit, and battery discharge rate analysis.

Thermal Service

Internal dust cleaning, fan lubrication, and high-performance thermal paste re-application ensure cool, silent, and throttling-free performance.

Grading & Sanitizing

Exterior ultrasonic cleaning, accurate cosmetic grading (Grade A / A+), genuine OS activation, driver updates, and secure dispatch packaging.
